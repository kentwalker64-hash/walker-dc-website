Who We Are

Federal Experience. Personal Commitment.

Walker Development & Construction LLC, doing business as Walker DC, is an SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business focused on federal commercial real estate brokerage and advisory services.

Founded by a retired U.S. Army aviator and former Department of Veterans Affairs senior real estate professional, Walker DC combines operational discipline with firsthand knowledge of federal leasing, acquisition requirements, and the facilities that support veteran healthcare.

We bring a mission-first approach to every assignment, with integrity, accountability, and attention to the details that determine successful outcomes.

Kent E. Walker, Founder & Principal

Kent Walker brings more than 21 years of combined federal and commercial real estate experience to Walker DC. Before retiring from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, he served as a Senior Realty Specialist and Project Manager, supporting major medical leasing initiatives nationwide encompassing more than 1.17 million ABOA square feet and an aggregate congressionally authorized contract value exceeding $1.18 billion.

During his final eight years with VA's Office of Construction and Facilities Management, Office of Real Property, Lease Execution Division in Washington, DC, Kent worked on micro-hospitals, outpatient clinics, residential rehabilitation treatment programs, and research and development facilities. His work extended from strategic capital investment planning through post-award design, tenant improvements, construction, change orders, lease acceptance, activation, and administration.

As a former VA Lease Contracting Officer and senior leasing leader, Kent brings firsthand understanding of federal procurement, negotiation, cost and price analysis, and best-value real estate solutions that support the care of veterans and their families.

A service-disabled veteran, Kent served in both the U.S. Marine Corps and U.S. Army over a 20-year military career. He was an AH-64 Apache instructor pilot, earned Senior Aviator, Airborne and Air Assault wings, and received the Army AH-64 Top Gun Award.
